Two-step sign-in

{%- if factors.enabled() %}

On. Signing in with your password also asks for a code from your authenticator app or a security key, and a passkey signs you in on its own. Apps you signed in to stay signed in; Subsonic apps sign in with an API key or app password, not your password.

{%- else %}

Off: your password alone signs you in. Add an authenticator app, a passkey or a security key and signing in will ask for it too; a passkey can sign you in on its own. Turning it on signs out every device, key and app password you have, so nothing signed in with the password alone stays signed in.
