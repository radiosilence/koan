- **Terminal UI: covers sized to the terminal's cells.** Covers and the tiles around them take their shape from the cell size the terminal reports instead of assuming a cell twice as tall as wide, so a tile no longer has a blank row under its cover and does not change height when it turns from half blocks into an image. Stopping after a long scroll turns the grid's tiles into images a row at a time rather than all in one frame, and iTerm2 keeps its images while scrolling.
