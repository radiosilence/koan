- **The keyboard in the kōan GUI app.** On a shelf, the search results and the downloads page the arrows bring the item they reach into view, a pill or a record tile included, and pressing a key that stays on the same item still does; the menu key opens under the item from where it was laid out. The page's list takes the keyboard back when the shortcuts or the artwork viewer close, after Back from its filter field, and after a click on the sidebar's place already showing; a click on the transport leaves it with the list. Tab stays inside a question or menu, and F6 waits until it closes. The cursor stays on its row when History gains plays or a list reloads, ⇧-clicks and ⇧-arrows extend from where the pick began, and the sidebar scrolls to follow its arrows.
