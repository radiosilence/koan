- **Seeks and playlist reorders.** The engine no longer spins at full CPU after a seek on a controlled device lapses or races a change of device, and a seek on a device that is controlled but not listed just now holds that device's playhead rather than snapping back. A seek the player never takes gives the playhead back to every front end, and the terminal UI's seeks and media keys land on the bar at once. A playlist reorder no longer waits for an earlier reorder's request to a slow server, so the transport stays responsive.
