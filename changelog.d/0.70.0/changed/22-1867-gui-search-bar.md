- **One search box in the GUI app.** ⌘K, `/`, ⇧⌘K and the sidebar's search field all open a search box centred over the window, which finds tracks, records and artists as you type and does everything from the keyboard: play or open the lit result, play it next, add it to the queue, tick several and act on them together, or open every result on a page of its own. It replaces the ⇧⌘K picker.
