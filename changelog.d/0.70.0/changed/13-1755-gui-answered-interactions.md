- **The GUI app answers what you do.** A heart filling sends out a few small dots and one emptying draws a ring closing in; the transport's buttons and every glyph button leave a faint ring as they are pressed; bordered buttons lift for a moment; a segmented choice, a field taking the keyboard and a sidebar row taking a drop draw a line under themselves. The sidebar and the lyrics panel slide in and out rather than snapping. Each lasts a quarter of a second at most, costs nothing at rest, and none plays under Reduce Motion.
