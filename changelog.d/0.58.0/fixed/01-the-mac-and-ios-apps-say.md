- **The Mac and iOS apps say when a command to another device is waiting or did not arrive.** "Waiting for Phone to wake" when it was queued for a device asleep, and "Couldn't reach Phone" with the reason when it was refused or failed. Moving the music goes by the answer to the Play it sends: there, waiting for a device asleep, or refused, in which case the music plays on where it was. A device that predates answers is still watched for the track, as before.
