- **Corrections from a measurement are squig.link's Auto EQ.** kōan fits a correction built from a measurement as squig.link's Auto EQ does: peaking bands below 6 kHz, boosting by no more than 12 dB anywhere, and a preamp, kept as the correction's bands and fitted again when its target changes. From the same measurement and target it plays what a preset exported from the site plays. Corrections saved from a measurement by 0.60.6 or earlier are fitted the same way when they play, so they no longer correct above about 6 kHz: 0.60.6 applied up to 12 dB there up to 10 kHz and up to 3 dB above it, so on most earphones the treble changes by several decibels.
