- **Push through kōan's relay.** Every server, self-hosted or not, now wakes iPhones, asks them to play and updates their Live Activities through kōan's push relay at `push.koan.rocks`, instead of only servers holding the developer's APNs key. The app registers with the relay, attesting with App Attest, and gives servers the relay's handles, never its device token; the relay decides what a phone shows. `[push]` is now `enabled` and `relay` (on by default; `enabled = false` keeps a server off the relay, and its apps ask for no notifications), the `koanApns` extension is `koanPush`, and the APNs key settings and `/push/cover` are gone. Device tokens the server kept are dropped.
