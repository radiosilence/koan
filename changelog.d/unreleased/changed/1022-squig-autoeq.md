- **Corrections from a measurement are squig.link's Auto EQ.** kōan fits a correction built from a measurement as squig.link's Auto EQ does: peaking bands below 6 kHz, boosting by no more than 12 dB anywhere, and a preamp, kept as the correction's bands and fitted again when its target changes. From the same measurement and target it plays what a preset exported from the site plays. Corrections saved from a measurement by earlier versions play the bands fitted to them in the same way; what they play changes by about 0.3 dB up to 10 kHz, and up to about 1 dB above, where the old curve still faded out.
