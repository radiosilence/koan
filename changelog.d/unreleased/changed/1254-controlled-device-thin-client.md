- **A device out of reach stays controlled, and says so.** A command for the device being controlled that cannot reach it now fails and says why, instead of playing on this device and dropping control; pick this device under Control to play here. The Control button turns to a red warning while nothing can reach the controlled device, before anything is pressed. Control Centre, the media keys and Android's media notification follow the controlled device's track changes even for tracks this library does not have. On a phone controlling another device, a call, Siri or unplugged headphones no longer pause or resume that device, and Android no longer takes audio focus from the phone's other apps.
