- **The desktop app is kōan on the Mac.** It takes the SwiftUI app's place, which is no longer released: its first launch on a Mac the SwiftUI app ran on carries on as that Mac, with its play history, TV pairings and saved queue. Quit the old kōan app before opening the new one; opened afterwards, it shows as "(legacy)". `Koan.dmg` and the Homebrew cask are the desktop app.
