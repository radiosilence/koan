- **The desktop app is kōan on the Mac.** It takes the SwiftUI app's place: its first launch on a Mac the SwiftUI app ran on carries on as that Mac, with its play history, TV pairings and saved queue. Quit the old kōan app before opening the new one. The SwiftUI app, deprecated, then shows as "(legacy)" and ships once more as `Koan-legacy.dmg`; `Koan.dmg` and the Homebrew cask are the desktop app.
