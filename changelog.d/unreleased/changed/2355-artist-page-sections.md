- **Artist page sections.** An artist's records are grouped under Albums / Live Albums, Singles / EPs / Demos, Mixes / Mixtapes, Spokenword / Interview / Audiobook and Compilations, in place of EPs & Singles, Live and Other releases. A record with several types goes to the first of spoken word, mixes, compilations and singles it carries, so a mixed compilation is a mix; live albums sit with the albums, and soundtracks and broadcasts with them too.
