- **Mac: picked records keep their order, and browsers keep their filters.** Records dragged, played or queued together arrive in a predictable order: ⌘A and ⇧-ranges in the order the page shows them, single picks in the order they were picked. Dropping several onto the queue or a playlist no longer scrambles them. Leaving the album, artist, track or history list and coming back, through the sidebar or back and forward, finds it with the same filter and scroll position.
