- **Picked records keep their order, and browsers keep their filters.** On the Mac, records dragged, played or queued together arrive in a predictable order: ⌘A and ⇧-ranges in the order the page shows them, single picks in the order they were picked, and dropping several onto the queue or a playlist no longer scrambles them. Leaving a browser and coming back, through the sidebar or back and forward, finds it with the filter it was left with, on the Mac and iOS; on the Mac the track and history lists also return to where they were scrolled.
