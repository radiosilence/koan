- **Download cache size stays current.** Settings' "Using" figure was read once when the app started and kept that value, so on a phone left running it could read "Zero KB" over gigabytes of downloads. The engine now keeps the count as downloads land and trims or clears remove them, and Settings follows it on the Mac and iOS. It reads "0 KB" rather than "Zero KB", Clear is a separate button, and the notice after clearing goes after a few seconds.
