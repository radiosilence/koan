- **Apple apps behave better around renderers, covers and pairing.** A call or unplugged headphones no longer pauses or restarts the music while an iPhone plays to a UPnP renderer. A Navidrome server's stand-in cover is recognised by the server's own marks, so real art shared by several editions is no longer hidden. The offer to wake an Apple TV after signing it in finds that TV by its id rather than its name. The Mac saves its session when it quits rather than each time it loses focus, and an iPhone finishes its save before it is suspended. Album pages and the queue number discs by one rule, and a queued track on no record shows its own sleeve and artist.
