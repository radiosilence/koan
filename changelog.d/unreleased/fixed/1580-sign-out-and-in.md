- **Signing out and in again behaves.** Signing out clears the queue at once, rather than once the server's library has been deleted, which on a large library took a while. A server signed out of, or left for another, refused every sync until the app restarted, including the one signing back in started and the Sync button, and reported it as up to date. Signing in ran the automatic sync and the app's own after it, walking the library twice behind two progress rows; the Apple apps now draw one row, and the second sync walks only if the first did not. On iPhone the server banner sits under the navigation bar instead of over its back button, and Settings shows the account an invite signed in to rather than the sign-in form.
