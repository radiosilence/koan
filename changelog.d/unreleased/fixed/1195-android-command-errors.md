- **Playing when the controlled device is out of reach plays here.** A device controlled from this one and no longer reachable, by the network or the server, used to swallow every play: now a play, a skip or an addition to the queue plays on this device instead, stops controlling the other, and says so. Pausing or seeking it says it is out of reach. A server's own refusal, such as an expired key, is shown as the server gave it, and Android shows command failures where it showed none.
