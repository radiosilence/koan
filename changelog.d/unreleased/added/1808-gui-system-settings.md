- **The kōan GUI app follows the system's display settings.** Increase Contrast, or high contrast on GNOME, draws stronger text, thicker rules, an accent held to 7:1 and outlined selections; Reduce Transparency takes the wash away; GNOME's text scaling factor sets the starting text size, which the app's own setting multiplies; and the remaining animations stop under Reduce Motion. Each applies as the setting changes.
