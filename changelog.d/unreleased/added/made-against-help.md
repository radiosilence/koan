- **Made against explains itself.** For a tuning that does not say what it was made against, kōan offers the target its curve fits, such as Harman for a tuning without Harman's bass shelf, on the EQ chain and the tuning's page; it is set only when accepted. The tuning's graph draws what it adds on the correction in use, and on a phone each choice shows that curve, so a wrong one shows as a bass shelf doubled. The chain draws the target difference it plays.
