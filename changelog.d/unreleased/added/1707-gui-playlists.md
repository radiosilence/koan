- **Playlists in the GUI app.** A playlist's page lays out like the queue, grouped by record or flat, and is edited as the Mac app's is: reorder by dragging, remove, add by dropping tracks, records or other playlists, rename, delete, undo and redo, each change previewed first; new playlists from the queue, a pick or a drop on the sidebar, and export to M3U8. Smart and read-only playlists are shown as such and never edited.
