- **squig.link's Auto EQ in kōan.** A correction built from a measurement can be fitted as squig.link's Auto EQ fits one: peaking bands below 6 kHz and a preamp, kept as the correction's bands. From the same measurement and target it plays what a preset exported from the site plays. `koan dsp measure --fit squig` and `koan dsp squig --use-result N --fit squig`; the apps keep the graphic correction for now.
