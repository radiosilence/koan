- **The kōan GUI app meets the system.** Its desktop builds, for macOS and Linux, shows what is playing in Now Playing and MPRIS and takes the media keys; has the Mac app's shortcuts and menus, and a shortcuts sheet; queues files dropped on it or opened with it; runs once per configuration, handing a second launch's files to the first; and is a device of its own beside the Mac app and the terminal UI. Releases carry a DMG, AppImages and .debs for it. Signing in, changing a password and redeeming an invite now name the server key after the device as it presents itself (the name given on the phone, or the TUI's and the desktop app's own) rather than the hostname.
