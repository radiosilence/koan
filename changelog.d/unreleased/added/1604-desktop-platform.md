- **The desktop app meets the system.** koan-desktop, for macOS and Linux, shows what is playing in Now Playing and MPRIS and takes the media keys; has the Mac app's shortcuts and menus, and a shortcuts sheet; queues files dropped on it or opened with it; runs once per configuration, handing a second launch's files to the first; and is a device of its own beside the Mac app and the terminal UI. Releases carry a DMG, AppImages and .debs for it.
