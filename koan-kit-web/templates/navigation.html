{#- One listener for the page's links. A plain click on a link inside a marked region, to this origin and not to a fragment of the page shown, reads its address in place; a link whose own listener already took the click (a `Link::in_place`) is left alone. The pressed link is busy until the load settles, and the next press abandons it, since a request from this element cancels the last. Back and Forward read the entry's address in place, unless only the fragment moved. With a signal to follow, the address bar follows it, and the click leaves the address to the answer that sets it. -#}
